    Teacher tip（教師提示，預設收起）

    Taking a 1 kg weight off both pans is the same as subtracting 1 from both sides. Halving both pans is the same as dividing both sides by 2 (or multiplying by 1/2).

    After Activity 4.1, transposition of terms（移項）can be shown as a shorter way of writing that same both-sides step — not as a different rule, and not by dragging a term across the equals sign.

    Today the class still centres on “the same operation on both sides”. If a student changes only one side, let the scale tip and ask what happened.
